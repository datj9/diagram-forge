# Design influences

Diagram Forge is an original implementation. Its product direction was informed by
two MIT-licensed projects:

- `cathrynlavery/diagram-design` — broad editorial diagram vocabulary and
  brand-conscious output.
- `tt-a1i/archify` — typed diagram specifications and verification-oriented
  technical diagrams.

No source code, templates, assets, or generated output from either project is
included in this repository. If future changes copy material from either
project, retain the corresponding MIT copyright and license notice.
